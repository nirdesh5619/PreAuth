# Cigna Injectafer (J1439) — fixture

Payer: Cigna
Policy ID: cigna-j1439-injectafer
Procedure: Ferric carboxymaltose (Injectafer)
CPT/HCPCS: J1439

## Coverage criteria

1. Laboratory confirmation of iron deficiency (ferritin below plan threshold or iron sat <20%) within the last 60 days.
2. Diagnosis of iron deficiency anemia or iron deficiency in heart failure / CKD as applicable, with ICD documented.
3. Documented trial of oral iron for at least 4 weeks, or a stated contraindication / intolerance / malabsorption.
4. Hemoglobin value within 30 days of the request.
5. Requested units align with labeled dosing (typically two 750 mg infusions at least 7 days apart for patients ≥50 kg).
