# Aetna Infliximab (J1745) — fixture

Payer: Aetna
Policy ID: aetna-j1745-infliximab
Procedure: Infliximab infusion
CPT/HCPCS: J1745

## Coverage criteria

1. Confirmed diagnosis of moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn disease, ulcerative colitis, or plaque psoriasis, documented in the chart.
2. Tuberculosis screening (IGRA or skin test) completed within 12 months prior to initiation, with result documented.
3. Failure, contraindication, or intolerance to at least 12 weeks of methotrexate or another conventional DMARD, unless the labeled indication does not require step therapy.
4. Requested dose does not exceed FDA labeling (typically 5 mg/kg at weeks 0, 2, 6, then every 8 weeks for IBD; 3 mg/kg for RA unless escalated with documentation).
5. No active serious infection at the time of request.
