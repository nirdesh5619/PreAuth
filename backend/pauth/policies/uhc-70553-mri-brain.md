# UHC MRI Brain with and without contrast (70553) — fixture

Payer: UnitedHealthcare
Policy ID: uhc-70553-mri-brain
Procedure: MRI brain with and without contrast
CPT/HCPCS: 70553

## Coverage criteria

1. Clinical documentation of a neurologic symptom or finding that MRI is expected to evaluate (new deficit, seizure, suspected mass, MS surveillance, or similar).
2. Recent relevant examination by the ordering clinician, dated within 90 days.
3. Conservative evaluation already performed when headache is the sole indication (history, neurologic exam, and consideration of CT or watchful waiting as appropriate).
4. Contrast is justified (known or suspected mass, infection, demyelination, or postoperative assessment), not requested by default.
5. No absolute MRI contraindication documented (non-MRI-conditional implant) unless a clearance note is attached.
