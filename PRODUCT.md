# Prior Auth Console

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

A local prior-authorization workbench. A coordinator submits a case, watches specialist agents gather evidence and match payer criteria, then approves or sends the case back before a letter of medical necessity is assembled. It does not submit to payers.

## Users

Clinic prior-auth coordinators and engineers demoing the agent graph on a laptop, typically at a desk in daylight, one case at a time.

## Brand

Quiet clinical operations. Paper, ink, and copper. Trust comes from citations and a visible graph, not from medical-teal chrome.

## Tone

Direct, unhurried, specific. Name the agent and the criterion. No hype, no emojis, no "AI-powered" copy.

## Anti-references

Hospital-portal teal, dark observability cockpits, purple SaaS gradients, Inter-on-slate dashboards, glassmorphism, hero metrics.
